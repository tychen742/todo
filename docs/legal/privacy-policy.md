## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

- **Account information:** your email address, display name, and password. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we cannot see them.
- **Profile information:** an optional profile photo or avatar choice, and an optional status message.
- **Workspace content:** todos and their details (text, notes, priority, due dates, scheduled times, estimates, workflow status, and archive state), projects, project phases and columns, milestones, mind maps, comments, and organization and team names.
- **Collaboration information:** your memberships and roles in organizations, teams, and projects; tasks you assign to others or that are assigned to you; and email addresses you enter to invite someone to a project or organization.
- **Support communications:** information you send us when you contact support.

### 2.2 Information from sign-in providers

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile information (such as your name) with us so we can create and identify your account. We do not receive your Google password. Google's handling of your information is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

### 2.3 Information collected automatically

- **Session and security data:** authentication tokens that keep you signed in, stored in your browser's local storage or your device's app storage.
- **Technical and log data:** when you use the Service, our hosting and database providers automatically record standard request information such as IP address, browser and device type, timestamps, and error details. We use this data to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

### 2.4 Information that stays on your device

Some features store data only on your device and do not send it to our servers:

- your theme and display preferences;
- calendar day notes;
- a local copy of workspace notes, used to load them quickly;
- the workspace activity timer and the browser focus indicator shown in the app header.

Clearing your browser or app storage removes this data.

### 2.5 What we do not collect

We do not currently use advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies. We will update this policy before adding any such tools.

## 4. How Information Is Shared

### 4.1 With other users

RodoFlow is a collaboration tool, so some information is visible to other users:

- **Members of shared spaces** can see the content in organizations, teams, and projects you belong to, along with your display name, profile photo, and status message.
- **People you assign work to, or who assign work to you,** can see the assigned task and who assigned it.
- **Other signed-in users can find you** by your email address or display name when adding members or assigning work, and can see your display name and profile photo in the results.
- **Profile photos** are stored at a publicly accessible web address so they can load quickly. Anyone who has the exact address can view the image. Do not upload a photo you would not want to be public.

Personal todos that are not in a shared space and not assigned to anyone are visible only to you.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete content, it is removed from the active database; archived items remain until you delete them.

You can delete your account at any time in **Settings > Your data > Delete account**. Deletion takes effect immediately:

- your account, profile, profile photos, personal todos, mind maps, and comments are deleted;
- organizations, teams, and projects you created pass to another owner or administrator of that space when there is one, and are otherwise deleted for all members (the app lists these spaces before you confirm);
- todos you created inside a shared space that continues, or assigned to another person, are kept and passed to that space's owner or to the assignee, so other people do not lose their work; and
- tasks other people assigned to you remain with them, with you removed as the assignee.

Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups until they are overwritten on a rolling basis within [BACKUP RETENTION PERIOD]. We may also keep limited information where the law requires it or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.
